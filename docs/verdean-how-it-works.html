The commercial reconciliation loop

From scattered promises to review-ready exceptions.

Companies run on quotes, contracts, invoices, emails, and conversations. Verdean connects that evidence, compares what was promised with what will be signed, and sends only the meaningful mismatches to a person.

How the work changes
Manual risk Agentic workflow Human decision
1

The company has a “Jack problem.”

Jack understands both the business and the systems. He knows where the Salesforce quote lives, which SAP document governs, and which email or call changed the deal. That expertise is valuable—but the entire reconciliation process depends on his time and memory.

2

The evidence refuses to stay structured.

A quote may be a clean record, a spreadsheet, a PDF, or words spoken on a call. A contract may use different labels, units, dates, or formatting. Manual reviewers must normalize all of it before they can even ask whether the records agree.

3

Verdean makes the loop agentic.

Verdean gathers the evidence, classifies and safely organizes it, links the likely governing contract, compares normalized values, persists the bounded review trail, and explains only the exceptions. The person no longer hunts for mismatches; they inspect annotated source clauses and review a grounded decision packet.

Narrative

Verdean does not replace Jack’s judgment. It removes Jack’s scavenger hunt.

Every company makes commercial promises across quotes, contracts, invoices, purchase orders, emails, and calls. Today, someone like Jack has to follow those promises across systems, translate inconsistent formats, and manually verify that the final contract reflects what everyone agreed. Verdean turns that expertise into a visible, bounded workflow: it builds the evidence set, organizes safe copies, matches the governing contract, detects term-level mismatches, persists review state, and presents annotated source clauses behind each exception. The agent does the repetitive reconciliation; the reviewer keeps control of the consequential decision and any email handoff.

Product truth The current MVP watches a user-approved local folder while the tab is open; it does not yet connect directly to Salesforce or SAP. Those systems appear above as the intended connector model. Verdean surfaces commercial exceptions for human review, stores bounded state in local D1 development, and can open a prefilled reviewer email draft. It does not provide legal advice, edit contracts, attach source files, send email automatically, or change ERP records.
